Supersedes DEC-099 on one point: the form linker cannot be a shell package. A flake's source copy has no parent directory, so `builtins.readFile ../bin/dev-envs-forms` cannot resolve, and a wrapped script also cannot locate the flakes tree from `$0` (it would resolve inside the wrong store path). The linker is therefore `bin/dev-envs-forms` in this repo, invoked by path like `bin/dev-checkouts`, tested by the repo's bash harness, and documented — not carried by the shell. Everything else in DEC-099 stands.
